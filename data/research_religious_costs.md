# Religious Burial Cost Research — for optional calculator toggles
Research date: 2026-09-23. Purpose: sourced cost components so each tradition toggles
specific calculator line items (no multipliers). Every figure below has a source URL.
"Unsourced" means no reliable figure found — needs a disclaimer, not a guess.

## 1. Jewish burial

**Line-item changes vs. standard model:** REMOVE embalming (prohibited except rare
cases; refrigeration used instead). REMOVE public viewing/visitation (no open-casket
viewing in traditional practice; Sinai Chapel GPL notes they do not offer public
viewing without embalming). CASKET → plain all-wood aron (wooden pegs, no metal,
unlined; rabbinic guidance prefers the simplest). ADD tahara (ritual washing) +
tachrichim (linen shrouds). ADD shomer/shmira (watcher until burial). Flowers and
music not customary (modest savings). Timeline: burial ideally within 24–48 hours;
no funerals on Shabbat or holidays (Friday/Sunday/holiday surcharges apply at some
mortuaries, e.g., +$250 Chevra Kadisha LA).

**Sourced ranges:**
- Tahara + tachrichim: $395 (Chevra Kadisha Mortuary, Los Angeles GPL —
  http://www.chevrakadisha.com/documents/mortuary-price-list.pdf); $490 (Am Israel
  Mortuary, San Diego — https://irp-cdn.multiscreensite.com/970f15ce/files/uploaded/Complete%20Chapel%20breakdown%20with%20simple%20pine%20casket%20%281%29.pdf);
  NO CHARGE (Sinai Memorial Chapel, San Francisco GPL —
  http://www.sinaichapel.org/tools-resources/price-list-sinai-memorial-chapel.pdf);
  $250 non-members / no charge members (Beth Israel Cemetery, Bellingham WA —
  https://bethisraelbellingham.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/151/2016/04/Customary-Fees-for-Various-Burial-Services.pdf).
  → usable toggle range: $0–$500, heavily provider-dependent.
- Shomer: $295 one night / $440 two nights / $575 three nights or over Shabbos
  (Chevra Kadisha Mortuary LA — same PDF as above). Often done by family/friends
  at no cost (Anshe Emet, Chicago —
  https://www.ansheemet.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/Funeral-Plan-October-2017-Updated_1.pdf).
- Plain pine casket (aron): $985 (Am Israel San Diego); $995 retail, unfinished pine,
  no metal (https://discount-caskets.com/products/plain-pine-eco-friendly-casket);
  $1,295+ handmade (Etsy); $1,510 Batesville pine (Crawford, Nashville TN —
  https://www.meaningfulfunerals.net/fh_live/10500/10561/media/Service---Merchandise-for-the-Jewish-Community.pdf).
  → usable range: ~$985–$1,510 via funeral homes; ~$995 retail.
- Rabbi honorarium: $500 (Am Israel San Diego); ~$500 non-members, $0 for members
  (Beth Israel, Ann Arbor MI — https://www.bethisrael-aa.org/pre-planning);
  $200 members / $500 non-members donation to rabbi's discretionary fund
  (Beth Israel Bellingham). → usable range: $0–$500.
- Refrigeration (instead of embalming): $295 flat after 3 days (Chevra Kadisha LA);
  $495 (Am Israel San Diego); $425 (Sinai SF).
- Total funeral-home portion (excl. cemetery): $4,300 all-in Traditions Program
  (tahara, shmira, tachrichim, pine casket, coach, permits — Mount Sinai, Los Angeles
  — https://mountsinaiparks.org/what-is-tahara/); $6,408 itemized total (Am Israel
  San Diego); ~$5,600 incl. pine casket (Beth Israel Ann Arbor, 2025 prices);
  $6,500–$6,910 graveside packages (Crawford, Nashville); $7,620 funeral plan
  (Anshe Emet Chicago). Consumer guides cite typical $4,000–$6,000, up to
  $10,000–$15,000 with premium casket (https://personalfinance.costhelper.com/jewish-funerals.html).
- Cemetery (Jewish plots): San Diego — Home of Peace plots ~$9,000 each,
  digging/filling/labor ~$4,000, ~$20,000 all-in; El Camino Memorial Park plots
  $19,500 + labor ~$6,000 ≈ $30,000 all-in
  (https://www.sdjewishworld.com/2025/01/25/the-high-cost-of-dying-in-san-diego-county/).
  Ann Arbor — plot $2,200 (members), opening/closing ~$4,000, vault ~$1,200,
  gravestone from $3,500 (https://www.bethisrael-aa.org/pre-planning).
  NY/NJ metro — King Solomon Cemetery, Clifton NJ: resale listings $1,500–$2,800/plot,
  cemetery retail stated as $3,800 each
  (https://picclick.com/Two-2-Burial-Plots-Clifton-NJ-West-Ridgelawn-Jewish-Cemetery-King-377082017769.html);
  Beth Israel Cemetery, Woodbridge NJ: resale $1,899–$4,250/plot, retail stated
  $5,000 each (https://picclick.com/Jewish-Cemetery-Plots-Up-To-Four-In-A-264266415800.html);
  Beth David Cemetery, Elmont NY: $3,500 auction listing; Temple Emeth, Bergen County
  NJ: $1,200/grave for members + NJ-mandated Maintenance & Preservation Trust fee
  (https://www.emeth.org/cemetery). NJ adds a 15% perpetual-care transfer fee on
  plot resales (per the Clifton listing). → NY/NJ usable range: ~$1,200 (member) to
  ~$5,000+ retail per plot, before opening/closing.

**Unsourced / needs disclaimer:** no national designed survey of Jewish funeral
costs exists (all figures are provider/consumer-guide based); NY-metro retail plot
prices direct from cemeteries (only resale listings found); tahara cost varies
$0–$500 by provider/volunteer model — present as range with note.

## 2. Muslim burial

**Line-item changes vs. standard model:** REMOVE embalming. REMOVE viewing and
casket (simple wooden box or no casket where cemetery permits; body wrapped in
kafan shroud). ADD ghusl (ritual washing, often performed by community
volunteers at the mosque — frequently low/no cost) + kafan. ADD janaza prayer
(usually no fixed fee). Timeline: burial within 24–48 hours. Concrete vault often
still required by the cemetery (~$1,100, MCCK Lexington KY).

**Sourced ranges:**
- Ghusl + kafan + fridge use: $350 (Bayshore Masjid via Islamic Center of Melville,
  Long Island NY — https://icmny.org/funeral/); washing materials ~$150
  (Jacksonville FL checklist — https://www.scribd.com/document/701582514/Bacaan-Muslim-Funeral-Guide,
  weaker source); community-performed at no stated charge (Charlottesville VA ISCV —
  https://charlottesvillemasjid.com/uploads/5/8/4/7/58472851/burial_information.pdf).
  → usable toggle: $0–$350.
- Complete burial totals: $5,156 itemized (plot $1,000 + opening/closing $650 +
  concrete vault $1,100 + marker $850 + funeral home $1,550 — MCCK/JFH, Lexington KY,
  Sept 2025 price list — https://mccky.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/MCCK-Price-list-9-3-2025.pdf;
  MCCK recommends budgeting $5,500); ~$3,500 all-in excl. headstone (Islamic Center
  of Nashville — https://www.icwctn.org/services/funeral/); ~$3,700 (Jacksonville FL;
  grave lot $2,400, free for mosque members); ~$5,500–$6,000+ (Naples FL —
  https://icnaples.org/services/funeral-arrangements/); Long Island NY ≈ $12,000+
  itemized (plot $8,000 + opening/closing $1,830 + liner $865 + ghusl $350 +
  transport $1,095–$1,820 — https://icmny.org/funeral/).
- Standalone burial fees: $2,200 adult / $1,000 child (AMAA Muslim Cemetery, Virginia
  — https://www.amaacemetery.org/burial-service/pricingtiming/); $2,421–$2,800
  (Monticello Memorial Gardens via Charlottesville ISCV); $1,500 to mosque
  (grave + kafan + cover + coordination $1,150 + digging $350 — Muslim Society of
  Memphis — https://muslimsocietyofmemphis.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/msmBurialPolicy_06072021.pdf).
- Transport: $700–$1,000 estimated (Charlottesville); $1,095 Suffolk / $1,295 Nassau /
  $1,820 NYC (ICM NY).
- Imam fee: "based on your appreciation" (ICM NY) — no fixed range sourced.

**Unsourced / needs disclaimer:** no national Muslim burial cost survey; kafan
material cost alone; imam honorarium range; figures vary 4x+ between Kentucky
(~$5,200) and Long Island (~$12,000+) — always present with location caveat.

## 3. Catholic burial

**Line-item changes vs. standard model:** essentially the standard traditional
model (visitation/viewing + Funeral Mass + burial). ADD church stipends
(priest, church use, music). OPTIONAL: mausoleum crypt in place of ground burial
(a common Catholic preference). Cremation permitted since 1963 — Funeral Mass may
precede cremation; ashes to be buried/interred (not scattered) per Church guidance.
No embalming/viewing changes.

**Sourced ranges:**
- Priest stipend: $100 suggested (parish cemetery fee schedule —
  https://uploads.weconnect.com/mce/bda09ba2c0046773a13bfac20bf620d2317adbf6/Cemetery/Cemetery%20Schedule%20of%20Fees%20Rev%203%20WORD.pdf);
  choir stipend $50–$100 suggested (same source).
- Funeral Mass packages: $1,000 parishioners / $2,000 non-parishioners, includes
  music (St. Vincent Ferrer, New York City, April 2024 policy —
  https://static1.squarespace.com/static/689b6197d3628b130d466ff7/t/68a52a513922f235b65569bc/1755654737663/SVSC-Funeral-Rites-and-Memorial-Mass-Policy-April-2024-For-Web.pdf);
  wake + Mass $1,250–$3,000 depending on parishioner status.
- Mausoleum crypts: single $7,000–$40,000 / double $10,800–$72,000, entombment fee
  $1,500 (Holy Cross Mausoleum, Archdiocese of Newark NJ —
  https://rcancem.org/pdfs/2025_HCMausoleumPriceDisclosure.pdf); indoor community
  crypt $7,000–$8,000 / outdoor $4,000–$5,000 starting (Dignity Memorial national —
  https://www.dignitymemorial.com/cemetery/mausoleum-costs); single crypt
  $4,300–$10,000, niche $1,100–$1,300 (St. Joseph Cemetery via weconnect schedule).
- Catholic cemetery ground plot with vault: $2,100 parishioner / $3,100
  non-parishioner (weconnect schedule).

**Unsourced / needs disclaimer:** no distinct national "Catholic funeral average"
survey — base remains NFDA medians + stipends above; music/organist fees vary
widely by parish.

## 4. Hindu rites

**Line-item changes vs. standard model:** cremation is the norm (not burial) —
model as cremation-with-ceremony. REMOVE embalming (quick cremation; refrigeration
if viewing). ADD puja ceremony before cremation (family typically provides samagri
items — coconut, ghee, flowers, cloth — nominal cost, tens of dollars). ADD
priest/pandit dakshina (gratuity). Witnessed cremation common. Ashes commonly
scattered or transported to India (transport cost significant — unsourced).

**Sourced ranges:**
- Hindu cremation packages: $3,356 Crematory Pooja Package, excl. crematory fee,
  permit, and priest fee (Boulevard Funeral Home, New Milford NJ —
  https://www.boulevardfuneralhome.com/hindu-cremation-package); $3,750 Puja
  Service Package incl. cremation permit + 2 death certificates (Hultgren, Carol
  Stream IL — https://irp.cdn-website.com/e0a10b26/files/uploaded/HFH-Puja-Services.pdf);
  $5,295 Hindu Cremation Service (Flintofts, Washington — older listing —
  https://flintofts-funeral-home-and-crematory.myshopify.com/blogs/cremation-selections/hindu-cremation-service);
  from $2,500 with fiberboard container / $3,000 with rental casket (Gordon Emerick
  via Hindu Temple of Albany bereavement guide —
  https://www.hindutemplealbany.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/Revised-BSG-Guide.pdf);
  Southern California $3,995–$7,775 (https://davidsguide.com/how-much-does-hindu-cremation-cost-in-southern-california-2/,
  marketing blog — weaker source).
- Priest/dakshina: $50–$300 (Albany temple guide, via imortuary blog — weaker);
  $200 recommended dakshina (Hindu death rites doc —
  https://www.scribd.com/document/375333880/Hindu-Death-Rites — weaker).
- Consumer guide range: $3,000–$20,000 depending on services/location
  (https://krishnabhaskar.com/hindu-funerals/ — community blog, weaker).

**Unsourced / needs disclaimer:** cost of transporting ashes or body to India;
puja samagri itemized cost; no national Hindu cremation survey. Present package
prices as examples with location labels.

## 5. Buddhist rites

**Line-item changes vs. standard model:** cremation common (burial also practiced).
Multi-day chanting/memorial services possible (venue/facility time). Monk offerings
(ofuse/dana) are donation-based, not fixed fees. 49-day memorial service is a
separate later cost.

**Sourced ranges:**
- Complete Buddhist traditional funeral + cremation package: $8,900 (Lotus Package,
  Baldi/Pennsylvania Burial Company, Philadelphia —
  https://www.pennsylvaniaburialcompany.com/lotus-package-buddhist-traditional-funeral-cremation-package-baldi-funeral-home).
- Temple donation schedule (Fresno Buddhist Temple, Jodo Shinshu — consistent with
  West Coast temples per the temple): funeral — temple $400 members / $600
  non-members; minister ofuse $250 / $300; organist $75 / $100; custodian $75 / $100.
  Cremation/burial service — temple $100 / $150; minister ofuse $100 / $200.
  49-day memorial — temple $100 / $150; minister ofuse $100 / $200
  (https://fresnobuddhisttemple.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/Subsequent-Memorial-Services-Homyo-and-Donations.pdf).

**Unsourced / needs disclaimer:** practices and costs vary hugely across
sub-traditions (Chinese, Vietnamese, Japanese, Tibetan); no national survey;
the $8,900 package is one provider's offering — label as example.

## Modeling notes for the calculator
- Jewish toggle: −embalming, −viewing, casket→plain pine (~$985–$1,510),
  +tahara ($0–$500), +shomer ($0–$575), rabbi honorarium ($0–$500), plot premium
  note for NY/NJ metros. Net effect: funeral-home portion often BELOW the
  standard $8,300 anchor (typical $4,300–$6,500), but cemetery costs can exceed it
  sharply in coastal metros — show both halves separately.
- Muslim toggle: −embalming, −viewing, −casket (or simple box), +ghusl/kafan
  ($0–$350), +vault if cemetery requires (~$1,100). Net: typically the
  lowest-cost burial path ($3,500–$5,500 in most markets; NY metro an outlier).
- Catholic toggle: +priest stipend (~$100), +Mass/church fees ($0–$2,000 by parish),
  optional crypt ($4,300–$40,000+) replacing ground plot. Net: at or above standard.
- Hindu toggle: force cremation-with-ceremony path (~$2,500–$5,300 package range),
  +priest dakshina (~$50–$300), −embalming, −burial plot/vault.
- Buddhist toggle: cremation path default; +temple/minister donations
  (~$175–$1,000); multi-day service note.
- Global disclaimer (all traditions): figures are provider/regional examples, not
  a national survey; requirements and costs vary by denomination, community, and
  metro; families should confirm with local clergy and funeral homes.
