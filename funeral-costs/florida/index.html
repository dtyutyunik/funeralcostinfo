Funeral Costs in Florida Modeled estimate

Quick answer: a traditional funeral with viewing and burial in Florida is modeled at $9,450 (range $8,030 – $10,870), versus the inflation-adjusted national anchor of $9,140 (the NFDA 2023 median of $8,300 in August 2026 dollars). Direct cremation is modeled at $3,130. These are modeled estimates — not surveyed prices or quotes — built from the NFDA 2023 national medians, inflated with the BLS funeral-expenses CPI, and adjusted by Florida's BEA 2024 regional price parity (103.4).

Florida in context

  • Florida runs modestly above the national average price level (RPP 103.4).
  • The NFDA projects 79.8% of Florida dispositions will be cremations by 2035, reflecting the state’s large retiree population and transient communities.

Local caveats

  • Florida regulates preneed (prepaid) funeral contracts through the Department of Financial Services; if you are considering prepaying, verify the seller’s license.
  • Prices differ between South Florida metros and the Panhandle — treat the state figure as a midpoint, not a local quote.

What's included — and what isn't

Included (per the NFDA median definition): basic services fee, removal/transfer, embalming and preparation, viewing and ceremony facilities/staff, hearse, service car, memorial printed package, and casket (burial) or cremation casket and urn (cremation with service).

Not included: cemetery plot, opening and closing, headstone or marker, burial vault (except the burial-with-vault row), flowers, obituary notices, and cash-advance items such as clergy honoraria or death certificates.

Frequently asked questions

Are these real prices from Florida funeral homes?

No. These are modeled estimates: the NFDA 2023 national median, adjusted to August 2026 dollars with the BLS funeral-expenses CPI and scaled by the BEA 2024 regional price parity for Florida (RPP 103.4). No state-level funeral price survey exists. Treat the point figure as a planning midpoint and the range as the plausible spread — then request itemized General Price Lists from local funeral homes for real quotes.

What does the $9,450 traditional-burial estimate include?

It follows the NFDA median's definition: basic services fee, removal/transfer, embalming and preparation, facilities and staff for viewing and ceremony, hearse, service car, memorial printed package, and a metal casket. It does not include the cemetery plot, opening and closing fees, a headstone, flowers, or obituary notices.

What is NOT included in these estimates?

Cemetery costs (plot, opening/closing, marker), flowers, obituary notices, clergy honoraria, death certificates, and other cash-advance items. Cemetery charges alone commonly add several thousand dollars to a burial. Use the calculator to add typical cemetery and cash-advance costs.

How accurate is the modeled estimate for Florida?

It is a transparent starting point, not a quote. The ±15% illustrative range reflects typical within-state variation; individual funeral homes can fall outside it, especially in high-cost metros or with premium merchandise. The model's accuracy improves as a planning anchor, not as a prediction of any single provider's price.

How do I get an exact price in Florida?

Under the FTC Funeral Rule you have the right to an itemized General Price List from any funeral home, price information by phone without giving your name, and to buy only the goods and services you want. Call or visit two to three providers, compare their GPLs line by line, and never sign under time pressure. See our guide to your rights under the FTC Funeral Rule.
