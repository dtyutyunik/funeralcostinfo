Section: Executive decision brief
Claim: FuneralCostInfo is worth a tightly scoped Phase 0 because the monetization ceiling is attractive, the data is public, and the product can be mostly static; the tradeoff is a deliberate 120-day SEO validation window.
Source: Owner brief; NFDA 2023 GPL study; BEA RPP release.

Section: Competition leaves an independent-data opening
Claim: Existing winners are directories, funeral-industry-funded marketplaces, broad planning platforms, or affiliate publishers; none combines consumer-only positioning, transparent modeled estimates, and an interactive estimator.
Source: Parting/Parting Pro, Funeralocity model, Foundation Partners/Cake, Precoa/Everplans, owner SERP review.

Section: The product is a calculator plus a transparent data system
Claim: The defensible asset is the reproducible estimate pipeline and printable calculator, supported by state pages and plain-language rights guides.
Source: Owner brief; NFDA, BEA, FTC.

Section: Public sources can support every launch estimate
Claim: NFDA provides national and regional anchors, BEA provides state price-level adjustments, FTC-compliant price lists can calibrate estimates, and BLS supplies trend context.
Source: NFDA 2023 GPL study; BEA RPP; FTC Funeral Rule; BLS CPI.

Section: The calculator must expose assumptions rather than pretend to quote a provider
Claim: Each result should show included and excluded items, source dates, model type, and a printable line-item range.
Source: Owner brief; FTC rule; NFDA scope notes.

Section: A static architecture keeps operations near zero
Claim: Pre-rendered Next.js pages on Cloudflare Pages, versioned JSON/MDX, and one annual data job avoid a database and routine support burden.
Source: Owner brief.

Section: Technical SEO and AI-search citation are one architecture
Claim: Sectioned sitemaps, semantic answer-first HTML, pre-rendered facts, consistent source strings, structured data, and deliberate AI-bot access maximize both crawlability and citation.
Source: Owner brief.

Section: Monetization works only if trust outranks yield
Claim: Restrained display ads create a floor; disclosed insurance leads create upside; funeral-home payments and dark patterns would undermine the differentiation.
Source: Owner brief; FTC endorsement guidance.

Section: Distribution must be engineered around original data
Claim: Calculator/map assets, annual state reports, local-news pitches, nonprofit resource pages, and advocacy partnerships are the white-hat path to links and citations.
Source: Owner brief.

Section: The build should advance in evidence-gated phases
Claim: Five states plus calculator and methodology are enough for Phase 0; 50 states, monetization, metro pages, and GPL sampling unlock only after indexed demand appears.
Source: Owner brief.

Section: The validation window is slower than the user's normal cycle
Claim: Search engine optimization cannot be judged in 14 days; 60-day impressions and 120-day traffic/ranking trends should govern continue, hold, or kill.
Source: Owner brief.

Section: Four owner decisions unlock the plan
Claim: Domain purchase, MVP scope, affiliate acceptability, and the 120-day exception must be approved before implementation.
Source: Owner brief.
