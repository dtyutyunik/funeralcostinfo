Executive decision brief

Build the data asset first; earn the right to scale

FuneralCostInfo is a credible low-operations bet because public data supports a useful estimator, demand is evergreen, and monetization can occur on a visitor's first session. The cost is patience: this project needs a deliberate 120-day search validation window, not a 14-day test.

Why it can win

  • Open position: no dominant, consumer-only funeral-cost data brand combines methodology, state estimates, and an interactive calculator.
  • Engineered economics: ads provide a floor; disclosed final-expense leads can provide materially higher yield.
  • Autonomy fit: static pages, versioned data, and an annual refresh avoid a database, account system, or support queue.
  • Owner fit: a senior engineer can build the defensible component - model, calculator, and publishing pipeline - rather than compete on article volume.

What must be true

  • Google and AI engines index and cite the original data experience.
  • Modeled estimates are labeled so clearly that criticism strengthens, rather than erodes, trust.
  • Monetization remains restrained around bereaved families.
  • The owner accepts a slower evidence loop than the normal 7-14 day rule.
Recommendation: approve a five-state Phase 0 only. Do not build all 50 states, buy links, or create a directory until Search Console shows real demand.

Phase 0 outcome

One production-quality home page, five state pages, the calculator, methodology, statistics, trust pages, and launch instrumentation. Owner involvement is capped at five hours for decisions, domain purchase, account approvals, and final review.

Market and competition

The opening is independence plus reproducible numbers

Demand is pain-driven and renews with each family; repeat visits are not required. Searchers arrive with immediate price questions, and every new visitor can generate an ad impression or a voluntary referral. The gap is not “more funeral content.” It is a transparent estimate system with no funeral-home paymaster.

Confirmed economic signals

  • Final-expense keyword research: roughly $8-$20 CPC for commercial terms.
  • Observed affiliate structures: $20-$60 per lead or $100-$300 per sale.
  • Lead buyers reportedly pay about $20-$45 for fresh final-expense leads.
  • Informational long-tail traffic often carries lower CPC, so affiliate intent matching matters.

Positioning rules

  • “Modeled estimate” appears beside every state number.
  • Never imply a provider quote or guaranteed local price.
  • Never rank funeral homes for payment.
  • Keep quote modules secondary to the answer.
  • Publish conflicts, affiliate relationships, and update dates.
Strategic tension: insurance commissions can fund the site, but an aggressive quote funnel would erase the independence claim. Treat trust as the primary conversion asset.

BLS funeral CPI

Inflation trend, not a state price source.[4]

Launch formula

stateEstimate = nationalMedian × (stateRPP / 100) Example only: $8,300 × (110.7 / 100) = $9,188 modeled estimate

California's 2024 all-items RPP was 110.7; Mississippi's was 87.0. BEA expresses the national price level as 100.[2] The example shows mechanics, not a provider quote.

Legal precision: the Funeral Rule requires accurate price information by phone and a written General Price List when in-person discussions begin; it does not universally require a funeral home to email a GPL. Sampling workflows must ask, not misstate the law.[3]

Calculator and content specification

The calculator exposes choices, assumptions, and exclusions

Inputs

  1. Location: state at launch; metro after validated demand.
  2. Service type: burial, burial with vault, cremation service, direct cremation, immediate burial, green burial.
  3. Optional items: embalming, viewing, ceremony, hearse, printed package, casket/urn tier.
  4. Known local prices: optional user overrides for cemetery or merchandise.

Outputs

  • Low / modeled center / high range
  • Printable line-item receipt
  • Included and excluded items
  • Source dates and model version
  • Questions to ask a funeral home
  • Optional, disclosed insurance quote link

Result logic

1. Load service bundle 2. Apply state RPP factor 3. Add selected options 4. Add user-entered local costs 5. Apply published uncertainty band 6. Render result + provenance 7. Track anonymous event only
No personal profile: calculations stay client-side. Do not collect a decedent's name, health information, beneficiary details, or exact address.

AI search and citation design

Write facts so humans can verify them and models can quote them

Simple “average cost” answers may be consumed inside AI Overviews. The counterplay is not hiding data; it is becoming the named source for complex, reproducible answers that assistants cite.

Answer-first page contract

  • First 40-60 words answer the exact query.
  • Use semantic h1, h2, tables, lists, and visible citations.
  • Repeat statistics consistently: “NFDA 2023 median: $8,300 for viewing and burial.”
  • Put key facts in pre-rendered HTML, never only after JavaScript runs.
  • Use FAQPage schema only when the same Q&A is visible.
  • Evaluate Speakable schema for concise answer blocks; never mark entire pages.

Machine-readable surfaces

  • /llms.txt - concise site purpose, methodology, canonical data pages, citation guidance.
  • /statistics/ - national facts, state table, definitions, source dates, downloadable CSV.
  • /methodology/ - formula, assumptions, validation sample, version history.
  • JSON-LD - Organization, WebSite, BreadcrumbList, Article, FAQPage where eligible.
  • Stable anchors - one URL and fragment per defined statistic or method step.

Deliberate crawler access

Dual risk

AI answers can reduce clicks on simple queries while increasing attribution for calculators, methodology questions, and state comparisons. Track both traffic and citations.

Measurability framework I

Track visibility first; revenue is a month-six metric

North-star metric: 30-day average organic sessions per day and its percentage change versus the prior 30 days. It measures whether search distribution is compounding without requiring daily work.

Launch instrumentation

  • Google Search Console: indexation, queries, pages, impressions, clicks, position.
  • Plausible or Umami: privacy-friendly sessions and events; GA4 as backup.
  • Rank tracker: 30 terms - 5 national and 25 state long-tails - checked weekly.
  • Core Web Vitals: field data plus deployment-time lab checks.
  • Affiliate events: UTM plus network sub-ID for page, service type, and CTA placement.
  • Ad dashboard: page RPM, viewability, earnings, policy notices.

Supporting metrics

  • Search impressions/day: earliest demand signal.
  • Tracked-set average position: visibility movement; report median too so outliers do not mislead.
  • Affiliate CTR: outbound quote clicks / eligible pageviews by page type.
  • Revenue: ads + accepted lead commissions, separately.
  • Referring domains: quality, relevance, and cumulative count.

Leading: indexed coverage, impressions growth, position movement, crawl frequency, referring domains. Lagging: sessions, accepted leads, RPM, revenue. Do not judge revenue before month 6.

Ordered miss-diagnostic - do not skip steps

  1. Technical: check indexation coverage, sitemap, canonicals, robots, noindex, rendered HTML, schema errors, mobile CWV, status codes. Fix and allow 14-30 days for recrawl.
  2. Content: compare search queries with page intent; test whether state pages are thin, repetitive, or omit inclusions, local rights, or direct answers. Upgrade the weakest five pages first.
  3. Competition: inspect the current top 10 for every tracked miss. Record new entrants, link gaps, information gain, and SERP features. Do not “add words”; add missing utility.
  4. Demand: compare actual impressions with the keyword thesis. If technically healthy, content-complete pages cannot generate impressions across the set, the demand estimate was wrong.

Monthly 15-minute scorecard

Auto-generate one page with: indexed/total URLs; impressions/day and 30-day change; organic sessions/day and change; tracked average and median position; count in top 3/10/20; quality referring domains; affiliate eligible views, clicks, CTR, accepted leads and EPC; ad RPM and revenue; total revenue; CWV pass/fail; open technical errors; next decision date.

Cadence

Weekly automated: rank snapshot, crawl error alert, uptime, affiliate event sanity check.

Monthly human: 15-minute scorecard review and one chosen action.

Stage gates: 30 minutes at days 60, 120, 180, and 365.

Interpretation: months 1-4 are judged on visibility. Revenue before month 6 is anecdotal and cannot rescue weak search signals or condemn a healthy ranking trajectory.
